📥 Data Input - Choose Your Method

How to load your data:

Option 1: Copy & Paste from Google Sheets

1. Go to your Google Sheet → Select the data you need (column headers + row data)

2. Copy (Ctrl+C or Cmd+C)

3. Paste in the text area below and click "Load Data"

Option 2: Upload CSV File

1. Export your data as CSV from Google Sheets

2. Select the CSV file below and click "Upload CSV"

Supported formats: CSV files from Google Sheets export
